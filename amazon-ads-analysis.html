What Your Amazon Ads Audit Covers

One audit, five outcomes. Together, they give you a full-funnel view: where customers enter, whether your channels are covered, and whether you're investing in the right places.

01

Market Share & Competitor Analysis

See exactly where you stand against competitors and the category, and where the opportunities are.

How we find it
  • Compare your sales and market share against named competitors.
  • Track category growth and new entrants.
  • Compare your organic ranking to category leaders on key terms.
  • Flag product opportunities you haven't launched, like a new size or variant.
02

Wasted Spend & Spend Efficiency

Find exactly how much ad spend is inefficient, and where to cut it.

How we find it
  • Compare spend and results by category and placement.
  • Check your budget split across ad types, and flag what's underperforming, draining resources, or missing your business goals.
  • Find search terms with clicks but no sales.
  • Audit your bidding strategy.
03

Missed Opportunities

Find the gaps in your strategy that could lift your sales.

How we find it
  • Find search terms converting well in broad or automatic campaigns, and move them into campaigns you control.
  • Find high-intent search terms you're barely advertising on.
  • Find products or categories with strong organic sales but little ad support.
  • Find easy wins, like cross-selling between your own products.
04

Profitability & Strategic Alignment

See if your ad spend is actually funding the business you want.

How we find it
  • Break down ad cost and return by keyword type, brand vs. generic.
  • Find which products give the best return, so you know where to invest.
  • Check if campaigns are built around your business strategy, not running on their own.
  • Check how dependent your sales are on branded search vs. new customers.
  • Check if you're only capturing today's demand, or also planning for growth and seasonal spikes.
05

Future Growth

See what your account could look like 3 to 6 months from now.

How we find it
  • Build a month-by-month growth plan, grounded in this audit's specific actions.
  • Model two paths: steady growth, or faster, higher-investment growth.
  • Set targets against your own numbers, not industry benchmarks.

Every pillar above comes with a plan, not just a finding. You get quick wins in the first 30 days, and growth moves over the next 90, mapped directly to the Future Growth projection above.
