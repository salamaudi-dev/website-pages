How Powerlook Recovered $105K+ a Month by Migrating Off a Platform That Kept Going Down

Powerlook’s Magento and React platform was bleeding roughly $105K every month in lost revenue through crashes and downtime. The question was not whether to move, but which platform could survive a festive peak, and whether a migration that size could run without taking the store offline.

Their story

A growing brand held back by the platform underneath it

Powerlook had demand. What it did not have was a storefront that could absorb it. The Magento and React stack crashed under load, and every hour of downtime came straight off revenue.

At roughly $105K a month in lost sales, the cost of staying put had already passed the cost of moving. The risk was that a migration done badly would cost even more.

The challenge

Replace the platform without losing a day of trading

Rescue a failing platform bleeding roughly $105K monthly, evaluate the best platform options through rigorous analysis, and execute a complete migration within three months with zero downtime.

Crashes under load
Revenue lost monthly
Festive peak approaching

Objectives

Choose the platform on evidence rather than preference, align every team on that choice before a line of code was written, and treat the migration as a conversion opportunity rather than a like-for-like port.

The solution

Evidence first, alignment second, migration third

Three moves carried the rebuild: a platform decision made on measured criteria, a planning phase that removed disagreement before the build, and a migration that carried a CRO roadmap with it.

01The call

The platform was chosen on measured criteria, not preference

Three candidates were compared on performance, scalability, and total cost of ownership.

03The real test

The festive season, on a platform three months old
