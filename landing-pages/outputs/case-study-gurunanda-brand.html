The challenge

GuruNanda had already built a US following around oil pulling. Taking that into the UK and Canada meant starting from nothing: no sales history, no reviews, no ranking, and an audience that in most cases had never encountered the category at all.

The obvious lever was price. It was also the one lever the brand would not pull, because discounting into a new market would have undercut the premium positioning that made the US business work in the first place.

The objective

  • Launch GuruNanda on Amazon UK and Canada from scratch
  • Educate international consumers on the specific benefits of oil pulling
  • Drive profitable global growth, balancing new-to-brand acquisition against retention in the US
The approach

How we ran it

Three moves, run in sequence rather than in parallel, so each one paid for the next.

Region-specific keyword strategy

Regional shopping behavior was read off Amazon Brand Analytics and Product Opportunity Explorer rather than assumed from the US account. UK and Canada got their own keyword sets, built around how those shoppers describe the problem, not how American buyers already describe the product.

Multi-format full-funnel campaigns

Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands video, and Sponsored Display ran together rather than as separate line items, with Brand Stores and Posts carrying the category education a cold market needs before a product page can convert.

Loyalty-driven scaling

Subscribe & Save turned first orders into recurring revenue. The two sides of the account then split: UK and Canada stayed on acquisition while the mature US business shifted toward lifetime value through remarketing and cross-sell.
