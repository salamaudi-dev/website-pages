## SECTION 5 — OUR SERVICES

## Our Services — The 7-Pillar GEO Framework

We start every engagement by benchmarking your AI citation share against the competitors you name — then work through all 7 pillars below to close the gap.

**LAYOUT: left-tab panel, 7 tabs (one per pillar), each tab body = 3 bullet points. Same tab-panel component as the Amazon/Shopify "Our Services" pages, repurposed here around the GEO framework instead of a cross-sell service menu.**

**01 — Grounding**
Building the authority and trust signals AI models check before citing a source.
- Expert-led content covering product fit, usage scenarios, and problem-solving — not generic marketing copy
- Author profiles, credible sourcing, and fact-check structures built into the page, not added as an afterthought
- E-E-A-T signals established at the content level, tied to the specific claims being made

**02 — Entity Structuring**
Defining your brand as a trusted, machine-readable entity.
- Organization, Product, and FAQ schema linking attributes, variants, and use cases
- HowTo schema for step-based content AI systems can extract directly
- Every schema deployment validated after it ships, not just installed and left

**03 — Predictive Coverage**
Covering every stage of the buying journey an AI model might be asked about.
- Full-funnel content — from "how to choose" guides to "which product fits which need" comparisons
- Discovery-to-decision mapping so no stage of the funnel is a citation gap
- Content roadmapped against actual query intent, not just keyword volume

**04 — Technical Setup**
Making sure AI systems can actually reach and parse your content.
- Verified crawl access for GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, and OAI-SearchBot
- Product, FAQ, and HowTo schema structured for direct AI extraction
- Core Web Vitals and page-speed fixes that remove the technical barriers blocking indexing

**05 — GEO Optimization**
Rewriting content specifically for how AI-powered search surfaces pull answers.
- Answer-first rewrites aligned to conversational, question-based queries
- Content restructured around how AI Overviews and chat interfaces actually phrase results
- Page performance checked against real prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude

**06 — Fan-Out Strategy**
Broadening your surface area so one page earns citations across many related queries.
- Use-case guides segmented by audience, activity level, or product type
- Comparison pages built around the attributes buyers actually query
- Synonym and related-query clustering so related searches route back to the same authority page

**07 — AIO Positioning**
Matching the exact formats AI Overviews and chat answers pull from.
- Step-by-step sections, bullet lists, and FAQs formatted the way AI Overviews structure results
- Direct Q&A blocks and comparison tables built into priority pages
- Pros/cons and list formats used wherever a buyer's query is inherently comparative

## SECTION 12 — FAQS

### What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and how is it different from SEO?

GEO structures your content, schema, and entity signals so AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews cite your brand directly in the answer they generate. Traditional SEO earns a ranking position on a results page; GEO earns a citation inside the answer itself — a page can rank #1 organically and still never get pulled into an AI Overview if it lacks the structural and trust signals the model needs.

### How do I know if my brand is already showing up in AI answers?

We run your brand and your named competitors through our proprietary AIO Citations Tool, which scrapes category queries across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews and reports the exact share of queries where each of you is cited. That gap analysis is part of the free audit.

### How long does it take to see results from GEO work?

Most engagements follow the same shape: Month 1 is a technical audit, query mapping, and initial content strategy; Months 2–3 move into technical implementation, backlink building, and pattern-based optimization; Month 4 onward is ongoing content and link building. Based on the case studies above, brands typically see the first AI Overview citations and non-branded click growth in the 5–8 month range, with 6–12 months being the realistic window for significant, compounding impact.

### Do you only work on AI citations, or also traditional Google rankings?

Both. Every GEO engagement includes the same on-page, technical, and off-page SEO work that drives traditional rankings — GEO layers on top of that foundation, it doesn't replace it.

### Which AI platforms do you optimize for?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and Grok, alongside Google AI Overviews — we track citation share across all of them every month.

### What does the free SEO & GEO audit actually include?

We review your current organic rankings, run your brand and named competitors through our AIO Citations Tool to see who's actually being cited, and audit your technical setup — crawl access, schema, and page speed — for the gaps blocking AI Overview inclusion. You leave the call with a prioritized fix list, not a generic pitch deck.

### How do you actually measure AI citation share?

We run your brand and the competitors you name through our proprietary AIO Citations Tool, which scrapes category queries across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews and reports the percentage of queries where each of you is actually cited. That gives you a real number to track progress against, not just a sense that AI mentions you more than it used to.

### Is GEO relevant for smaller or early-stage brands, or only enterprise?

GEO works best for brands that already have meaningful organic traffic and a real content footprint to restructure — as a rough guide, that's 50,000+ monthly site visits and an established product catalog. If you're earlier than that, foundational SEO usually needs to come first. Ask on the audit call and we'll tell you honestly which stage you're at.
