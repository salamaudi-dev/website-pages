Growisto homepage: three prototype directions

Same content spine in all three, so the comparison is about visual direction rather than copy. Every section is responsive and the tabs, accordions and carousels are live.

Type sizes, line heights, tracking and text colours are read directly from the Figma file. Imagery and client logos are exported from the same Figma file; the logos are drawn only from the permission-cleared lists in landing-pages/CLAUDE.md. Portraits are stock placeholders, not the people they are captioned as. Headline faces in A and B are substitutes: the Figma file uses Season Mix in trial form, which is not licensed for production.
