Fashion & Apparel · Website & Tech

Strategic migration that recovered $105K+ in monthly lost revenue

Brand
Powerlook
Market
India
Department
Tech
Timeline
Migration delivered in 3 months

Powerlook, a premium men’s fashion brand, was losing roughly $105K every month to crashes and downtime on its Magento and React platform. The question was not whether to move, but which platform could carry a festive-season peak without going down, and whether a migration that size could be run without taking the store offline.

Visual · storefront migration view
Result 01$105K+Monthly lost revenue recovered
Result 0280%Revenue growth vs the previous festive season
Result 0350%Reduction in operational costs

Strategic approach

  • Compared Magento Headless, Shopify Plus, and Shopify Plus Headless across performance, scalability, and total cost of ownership before committing to a platform
  • Aligned the tech, marketing, and management teams on the decision through multiple working sessions, so the build started with one agreed scope
  • Built a conversion-focused migration roadmap rather than a like-for-like port, folding CRO changes into the rebuild itself
  • Launched the full Shopify Plus platform in three months with zero downtime and zero bugs

The wiki record also shows an 11% conversion rate improvement post-migration and zero downtime through the cutover.

Consultancy · GEO, AIO & SEO

NDIS consultancy grew non-brand clicks 32x in twelve months

Brand
Avaana
Market
Australia
Department
GEO/SEO
Timeline
12-month engagement

Avaana is an Australian consultancy that helps businesses through NDIS provider registration and growth, covering application preparation, audit coaching, and business strategy. Almost all of its search visibility was branded. The work was to build non-brand demand around how operators actually search for NDIS regulation, registration, and provider services, and to be present when those questions get asked of an LLM rather than a search box.

Visual · non-brand click growth curve
Result 0132xNon-brand clicks, from 16 to 516 in 12 months
Result 0273%Core business keywords ranking in the Google top 10
Result 0313xReturn on investment from SEO and GEO in 2025

Strategic approach

  • Ran a full content strategy across more than 900 high-relevance keywords and mapped 62 unique blog pages against NDIS regulation, registration, and services intent
  • Rewrote and optimized 17 priority pages with highly targeted on-page work
  • Optimized page content to surface inside AI and LLM search results, not only Google’s organic listings
  • Completed a technical audit covering page speed, coverage issues, and site parameters, and ran targeted backlinking against the priority pages

The wiki record also shows 100+ LLM sessions contributing a 20% boost to organic traffic.
