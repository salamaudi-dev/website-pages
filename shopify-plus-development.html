Why Shopify Plus

Why Enterprise eCommerce Brands Choose Shopify Plus

Standard Shopify takes you far. Shopify Plus takes you further — with enterprise capabilities that unlock the next stage of growth without the infrastructure overhead of going headless.

Fast-growing eCommerce brands typically hit the ceiling on standard Shopify around the $1M–$5M annual revenue mark — not because of traffic, but because they can't customise the checkout, their marketing team can't make changes without a developer, or their catalogue and automation needs have outgrown the platform.

Shopify Plus solves this with Checkout Extensibility for custom checkout flows, Shopify Functions for custom backend logic, advanced automation, and multi-store management — all while keeping developer dependency low so your team stays agile.

FAQ

Common Questions About Shopify Plus Development

What's included in Shopify Plus development services?

+

Custom theme development, conversion rate optimization, Shopify app development, checkout customization, B2B store setup, headless implementations, enterprise integrations, and ongoing support.

How does Shopify Plus work?

+

Shopify Plus is the enterprise version of Shopify providing custom checkout extensibility, B2B functionality, automation flows, multi-store management, and dedicated merchant success support.

What's the difference between Shopify and Shopify Plus?

+

Shopify Plus offers enterprise-grade features not available on standard Shopify, including unlimited staff accounts, advanced API access, checkout customization, B2B native features, Shopify Flow automation, and dedicated support including a Launch Engineer.

How can Shopify Plus be leveraged for ecommerce business?

+

Shopify Plus enables high-growth brands to scale efficiently with multi-currency, cross-border selling, custom checkout flows, advanced automation, and B2B capabilities, all without the infrastructure overhead of headless commerce.

How do I find a reliable Shopify Plus developer?

+

Look for a Shopify Select Partner or Shopify Plus Partner with proven case studies showing measurable conversion improvements. Growisto is a Shopify Select Partner with 150+ completed projects.

How does Shopify Plus cost compare to other platforms?

+

Shopify Plus includes hosting, security, and support that would cost significantly more to replicate on custom platforms. When you factor in reduced developer dependency and lower ongoing maintenance costs, the total cost of ownership is often lower than Magento or custom headless builds.

What makes Growisto's Shopify Plus development different?

+

Every decision, from theme selection to each line of code, is made with conversion rate and page speed in mind. We bring CTO-level strategy to every engagement.

Can you integrate third-party apps and APIs with Shopify Plus?

+

Yes. We have experience integrating ERPs (SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics), payment gateways (Stripe, Razorpay, Adyen), fulfilment platforms, marketing tools, and custom APIs with Shopify Plus stores.

What is a Shopify Plus development agency?

+

A certified partner specialising in building, migrating, and optimising Shopify Plus stores for enterprise brands, with platform-specific expertise in Checkout Extensibility, Shopify Functions, B2B native features, and enterprise integrations.

What is Shopify Plus Checkout Extensibility?

+

Shopify Plus's framework for customising the checkout experience without modifying underlying checkout code. It allows upsells, custom form fields, order summaries, post-purchase pages, and thank-you page modifications using Shopify's native UI extensions.

Will AI replace Shopify developers?

+

No. AI tools are accelerating development speed but can't replace the architecture decisions, business logic, and CRO strategy that determine whether a Shopify Plus store converts. Our developers use AI tooling to move faster. Strategy and integrations still require experienced engineers.

How long does Shopify Plus development take?

+

Theme customisation or CRO-focused build: 4–8 weeks. Full new Shopify Plus store with custom integrations: 8–16 weeks. Headless Shopify Plus builds (Hydrogen & Oxygen): 16–24 weeks. Migrations from Magento or WooCommerce: 6–12 weeks depending on catalogue size.

What kind of support does Shopify Plus provide?

+

Shopify Plus includes 24/7 priority support, a dedicated Launch Engineer for migrations and new builds, and access to a Merchant Success Manager, in addition to the support Growisto provides as development partner.
